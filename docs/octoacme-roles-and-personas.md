# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

## QA/Testing Lead
### Role Summary
The QA/Testing Lead ensures product quality, validates acceptance criteria, and manages the testing strategy across the project lifecycle. They collaborate with developers and product managers to define quality standards and prevent defects from reaching production.

### Responsibilities
- Define testing strategy and test plans (unit, integration, end-to-end, performance)
- Create and maintain test cases based on acceptance criteria
- Execute manual testing and validate acceptance criteria compliance
- Coordinate with developers on test automation and CI/CD integration
- Report quality metrics, test coverage, and defect trends
- Participate in release verification and smoke testing
- Advocate for quality and identify technical debt related to testing
### Goals
- Achieve and maintain high test coverage and quality standards
- Catch defects early in the development cycle
- Ensure releases meet customer expectations and business requirements
- Support rapid iteration without compromising reliability
### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement sessions
- Daily standups with focus on blocker testing
- Test status reports and quality metrics
- Release readiness sign-off


## Stakeholder/Business Owner
### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Business Owners represent customer needs, business goals, and organizational constraints. They provide context, approvals, and prioritization guidance to ensure projects deliver measurable business value.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business requirements and success metrics
- Review and approve project charters and major decisions
- Communicate project status and outcomes to broader organization
- Identify and escalate business risks and dependencies
- Support user research and customer validation activities
- Participate in stakeholder reviews and milestone gates
- Help resolve trade-offs between scope, timeline, and quality
### Goals
- Ensure project delivers intended business value and ROI
- Maintain strategic alignment with organizational priorities
- Enable rapid feedback loops to validate product-market fit
- Reduce deployment risk and improve adoption
### Typical Communication
- Project kickoff and milestone reviews
- Weekly or bi-weekly status updates
- Escalation channels for blockers and decisions
- Post-release retrospectives and outcome measurement
## Technical Lead/Architect
### Role Summary
The Technical Lead/Architect designs technical solutions, ensures scalability and maintainability, and manages technical dependencies. They collaborate with product and project leads to align technical feasibility with business goals.

### Responsibilities
- Design technical solutions and architecture for features
- Identify technical dependencies and integration points
- Mentor developers on design patterns and best practices
- Participate in code and design reviews
- Assess technical risks and propose mitigations
- Plan technical debt reduction and refactoring work
- Collaborate with infrastructure and security teams
- Advocate for technical sustainability and long-term maintainability
### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, and secure solutions
- Reduce technical debt and complexity over time
- Enable team velocity and minimize rework
- Balance feature delivery with engineering quality
### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and RFC (Request for Comments) discussions
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement
- Architecture documentation and decision logs
- Technical mentoring and code review comments

## Scrum Master/Agile Coach
### Role Summary
The Scrum Master/Agile Coach facilitates process execution, removes impediments, and fosters a culture of continuous improvement. They support the team's adoption of agile practices and help resolve team dynamics issues.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint ceremonies (planning, standups, reviews, retrospectives)
- Remove process impediments and blockers
- Track team velocity and sprint metrics
- Coach team members on agile practices and self-organization
- Identify and escalate team dynamics or morale issues
- Manage project board and workflow hygiene
- Drive continuous process improvement based on retrospectives
- Collaborate with PM and Project Manager on capacity planning
### Goals
- Maximize team productivity and predictability
- Foster psychological safety and continuous learning
- Minimize process overhead and meeting load
- Support sustainable pace and prevent burnout
### Typical Communication
- Facilitation of all team ceremonies
- One-on-ones with team members to understand blockers
- Weekly metrics and process improvement recommendations
- Coaching conversations on agile practices
